ABSTRACT — Vision-Language-Action models (VLA) are saturating; the field is pivoting to World Action Models (WAM). We propose FactorWAM, a self-supervised reward framework derived from a video world model's imagined trajectory consistency. Rather than a single scalar reward, we decompose the signal into when / which / how components, stage training via a WAM-guided curriculum ranked by world-model prediction error, and augment inference with MCTS over latent rollouts. No proprietary VLM judges, no human preferences, no robot teleoperation. We validate on Open-X-Embodiment manipulation benchmarks via DreamDojo-style Real2Sim2Real rollouts and release EgoActionVideo-50K, a glove-pose-annotated YouTube corpus designed as a citation-magnet companion artifact.

Three Novelty Vectors

The base proposal uses one flat reward signal: r = −‖zt+H − g‖. The three techniques below transform it from “a paper about a new reward signal” into a paper about a new reward framework — decomposed, curriculum-aware, and search-augmented — all grounded in the same WAM backbone.

01

The Great Parallel

Jim Fan's thesis: the LLM playbook (Pre-Train → SFT → Reasoning RL) maps one-to-one onto the robotics stack (World Modeling → Action Fine-Tuning → Physical RL). Each row is "simulating," "aligning," then "surpassing." Robotics is doing in 2026–2028 what language models did in 2022–2024 — but the aligning step has no scalable reward signal yet. That is the gap FactorWAM fills.

LLM · Solved Pre-Training Web-scale text
→
LLM · Solved Supervised FT Instruction tuning
→
LLM · Solved Reasoning RL RLHF / GRPO / DPO
Robotics · 2024–25 World Modeling V-JEPA, Genie, UniSim
→
⚡ Gap · 2026 Action FT Where reward signals should live
→
Robotics · 2027+ Physical RL DreamDojo, parallel sims

Existing approaches plug this gap with proprietary VLM judges (GPT-5, Gemini) that are expensive, drift, and produce inflated scores. FactorWAM proposes a self-supervised alternative: let the world model judge itself.

~$500 · 6 wks

03

🎯 R1 Reward Model · Trajectory Consistency

~$200 · 3 wks

Score candidate actions by their imagined-trajectory consistency: roll out the world model under action a, measure whether the predicted trajectory reaches the linguistic goal embedding. Decompose the reward into Rwhen (act or wait?), Rwhich (grasp/push/pour?), Rhow (trajectory smoothness), and Routcome (goal distance). No robot, no VLM judge, no preferences.

04

🤖 R2 Policy · Real2Sim2Real Training

~$400 · 6 wks

Use Open-X-Embodiment public trajectories. Construct DreamDojo-style digital cousins via Isaac Sim / CARLA. Train via a WAM-guided curriculum (easy scenes first, ranked by WAM rollout error). Fine-tune OpenVLA or Pi-Zero with our decomposed R1 reward via GRPO. At inference, use MCTS over WAM latent rollouts to search over action sequences. Compare against teleop-only baselines and RoboReward-style proprietary judges.
